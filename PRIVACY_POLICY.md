# Privacy Policy for ELL-ena

**Last Updated:** July 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **ELL-ena** ("we," "our," or "us"), an open-source AI-powered workspace assistant developed by **AOSSIE**.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your data when you use the ELL-ena mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App").

By using ELL-ena, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.


# 2. Information We Collect

## A. Information You Provide

When you create an account or use the App, we may collect:

* Name
* Email address
* Profile information
* Team or workspace information
* Tasks, tickets, notes, and other content you create within the App
* Meeting links or scheduling information that you choose to provide

Authentication is securely managed through **Supabase Authentication**. ELL-ena does not store your password in plain text.


## B. Meeting and AI Processing Data

If you use meeting-related features, we may process:

* Meeting links
* Meeting transcripts
* AI-generated meeting summaries
* AI-generated action items
* Vector embeddings used to provide semantic search and AI-powered features

Where meeting recordings are used for transcription, they are processed only as necessary to provide the requested functionality.


## C. Automatically Collected Information

To improve reliability and security, we may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

* Device model
* Operating system version
* IP address
* App diagnostics
* Crash reports
* Performance logs

This information is used solely to improve the performance, reliability, and security of the App.


# 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

* Create and manage your account
* Authenticate users securely
* Enable collaboration within workspaces
* Process meetings and generate AI-powered summaries
* Provide semantic search using vector embeddings
* Respond to support requests
* Improve app functionality and user experience
* Protect the security and integrity of our services
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information.


# 4. Third-Party Services

ELL-ena relies on trusted third-party service providers to deliver its functionality.

These include:

### Supabase

Used for:

* User authentication
* Cloud database storage
* Secure backend services
* File storage
* AI-related backend processing

### Google Services

If you choose to connect Google services (such as Google Meet or other supported Google Workspace integrations), ELL-ena processes only the information necessary to provide those features.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or governmental request.


## Google API Services User Data Policy

ELL-ena’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the **Limited Use** requirements.


# 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

* Encrypted network communication (TLS)
* Secure authentication
* Row-Level Security (RLS) within our database
* Access controls to limit unauthorized access

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.


# 6. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the services described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When your data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.


# 7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

If you are unable to access your account or need assistance, you may submit a deletion request by contacting us using the email address provided below.

Once your request has been verified, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable period unless we are legally required to retain certain information.
